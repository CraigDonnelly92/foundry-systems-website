Elite Windshields

A glass shop, just like the businesses I build for.

What they needed: a professional site that makes it quick for drivers to get a quote or call the shop, and that shows off more than just windshields.

What was built: a dark, sharp site with "Get a Quote" and the shop's phone number pinned in the header, pages for every service (windshield replacement, rock chip repair, window tinting, commercial and fleet glass, Lexan and Margard sheet, and equipment cutouts), a photo gallery of real jobs, and trust points up front: 2-year installation warranty, mobile service, insurance claims and fast turnaround.

Worsley Baptist Church

Proof the same care works beyond the trades.

What they needed: one place where newcomers and members can find service times, what to expect, and what's coming up.

What was built: a calm, welcoming site with Sunday and Wednesday times right on the homepage, a "New Here" page for first-time visitors, pages for ministries, events and hall rental, a member login, and the latest sermon pulled straight from the church's YouTube channel.
