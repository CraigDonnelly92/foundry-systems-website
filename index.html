Common questions

Straight answers.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once your build is paid, the finished site is yours, and so is your domain name, your logo, photos and text. If you ever leave, you can take it with you.

How long does it take?

About 2 weeks from the day your project form and payment come in. If I'm waiting on photos or info from you, the clock pauses until it arrives.

Is hosting included?

Yes. Hosting is part of the care plan that comes with every site, along with updates and fixes. You won't get a separate hosting bill.

What do I need to provide?

Your business info, your logo if you have one, and photos of your work. The project form walks you through it. Anything you're not sure about, we'll figure out together.
